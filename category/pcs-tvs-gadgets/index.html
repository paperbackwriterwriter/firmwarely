PCs, TVs & gadgets: latest firmware updates

Laptops, motherboards, TVs, printers, headphones, cameras and e-readers all run firmware, and most of it updates from a settings menu nobody opens. BIOS releases close security holes, TV updates change what apps work, and camera firmware adds autofocus modes years after purchase. Firmwarely lists the current version and the manufacturer's notes so you know when it is worth the trip into the menu.
