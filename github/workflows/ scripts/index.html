    How it works

    1. 1

      Pick the devices you own

      Search the catalog for your router, NAS, cameras, console, drone or e-bike and add them to your list.

    2. 2

      We check every night

      Firmwarely reads each manufacturer's release page and changelog and records the version, date and what changed.

    3. 3

      You get one email

      A weekly digest on the free plan, or an instant alert on Pro the moment a security fix ships for something you own.
