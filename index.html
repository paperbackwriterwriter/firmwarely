    How it works

    1. 1

      Pick the devices you own

      Search the catalog for your router, NAS, cameras, console, drone or e-bike and add them to your list.

    2. 2

      We check every 30 minutes

      Firmwarely reads each manufacturer's release page and project feed around the clock and records the version, date and what changed the moment it appears.

    3. 3

      Pro members get one email a day

      Each morning, Pro members get a single email covering every device of theirs that changed in the last 24 hours, security fixes first. Free members see the same information here on the site.
