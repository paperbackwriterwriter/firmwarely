How to update the DJI Air 3

  1. Open DJI Fly, DJI Go or the app for your model with the aircraft and controller powered on.
  2. The app prompts when firmware is available; keep both connected and batteries above 50%.
  3. Alternatively connect to a computer and use DJI Assistant 2.
  4. Update every battery too — DJI batteries carry their own firmware and a mismatch blocks takeoff.

Always download firmware from the manufacturer or project itself. Firmwarely links out; it never hosts firmware.
